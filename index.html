Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 29, 2026

Welcome to Eisenmeer Games. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, enjoyable gaming experience. This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games, services, and applications developed and published under the Eisenmeer developer label, including our survival RPG title, Pixel Wasteland Courier.

Please read this policy carefully to understand how we treat information related to your use of our games.

1. Information We Collect or Store Directly

We do not collect or store any of your personal data directly.

  • We do not require you to provide personal details (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) to play our games.
  • Any game progress data, customized skins, unlock statuses, settings, and high scores are preserved 100% locally on your own physical mobile device. This data is not transmitted to us and can be cleared entirely by resetting or uninstalling the application.

2. Third-Party Services We Use

To support game development, optimize performance, and deliver personalized options, our applications may integrate certified third-party SDKs and service providers. These third-party services may automatically capture system specifications and non-personally identifiable identifiers. These service providers include:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Console: For secure app installation verification, performance telemetry, and general diagnostic crashes reporting.
    → Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Optional in-game monetization, ad delivery, and custom ad-campaign measurements. AdMob may utilize anonymous mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or IDFA) to personalize or measure ads.
    → Google Advertising Policy

3. Local Device Permissions

Our applications may request specific device-level permissions to ensure core gaming features operate flawlessly (such as vibration feedback or local game data backups). You hold absolute command over these permissions through your device's native System Settings and can toggle or disable them at any time.

4. Children's Privacy

Our mobile games are designed for general audiences and are fully safe for children. Because we do not directly collect, prompt for, or process any personal data or contact details, our services comply perfectly with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) guidelines.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe we have accidentally received any information, please reach out directly to our support contact listed below, and we will immediately handle the query.

5. Content Security

We prioritize the security of your interactive experience. Since your game profiles are backed up locally on-device, we recommend maintaining standard security guidelines (such as updating Android operating versions) to keep your local files safe from device-level breaches.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may upgrade this Privacy Policy dynamically as we publish new titles or update existing services. Any changes will be published directly inside this document with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of the interface. We recommend reviewing this document periodically to stay informed.
